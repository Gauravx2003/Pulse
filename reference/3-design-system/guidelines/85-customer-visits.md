# Customer visits and customer experience

Some engineers go on site to customers — installations, troubleshooting, escalations, workshops, demos. This is extra effort compared with colleagues who only do sprint work, so Pulse records it, asks the customer how it went, and credits it.

## Flow

1. **Plan or log** — the manager plans a visit for someone (or the engineer logs one): customer, city, purpose, dates, who requested it (manager, account manager, support lead, the customer), the **account owner** who will collect customer feedback, and a linked ticket or deal. Visit days are **taken out of sprint capacity**, so utilization shows the real load.
2. **Visit report** — after the visit the engineer submits what was done, hours on site, travel hours, outcome and follow-ups. Until then the visit shows *Report due* and the manager can send a reminder.
3. **Customer feedback** — the account owner (an Employee, e.g. sales or support) gets an in-app notification and records CSAT 1–5, whether the customer would ask for this engineer again, and what the customer said.
4. **Verify** — the manager verifies the extra-mile points (on the Customer visits screen or in Monthly effort). Verified points credit culture values.

Status: Planned → Report due → Feedback pending → Awaiting manager → Verified (or Not counted).

## Points (Rules & thresholds)

| Item | Default |
|---|---|
| Customer visit (report submitted) | 1.5 |
| Each extra day on site | 0.5 |
| Each weekend / holiday day on site | 1 |
| Escalation visit | 0.5 |
| Customer rated 4.5 or higher | 1 |
| Monthly cap for visit points (separate from the extra-mile cap) | 8 |

Values credited by purpose: deployment → Outcome Ownership, Adaptability · troubleshooting → Problem Solving, Outcome Ownership · escalation → Resilience, Problem Solving, Outcome Ownership · workshop → Collaboration, Adaptability · demo / POC → Collaboration, Risk Appetite · health check → Outcome Ownership, Collaboration · weekend days add Resilience.

## Compared with the team

Each person’s visits, days on site, weekend days, travel hours, visit points and customer CSAT are shown against the **team average** (new joiners excluded) as a bar with an average mark and a badge such as “2.0× team avg”. The team view lists everyone, including people with no visits, so the extra effort is visible. Visit days show as **Visit** in the team heatmap and the monthly daily log, and appear in the appraisal evidence.


The period chips are **Q3 2026**, **2026 to date** and **Month / sprint**, which shows the shared sprint or month selector.
