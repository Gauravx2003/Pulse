# Training check-ins and trainee feedback

Every training assignment has a **progress check-in cadence** — **Daily**, **Weekly** or **Monthly** — set on the plan (e.g. *Jira discipline refresher* = daily, onboarding = weekly) and overridable per assignment when assigning or editing.

## Trainee (any employee with a plan)

Anyone who is assigned a plan — a new joiner or an existing employee — sees it under **Training plans → My training**:

- Progress %, overdue modules, mentor, and a **Check-ins** tile (verified of total; *update due* or *missed*).
- **Progress check-ins**: one row per day / week / month since the start. Each shows *Due*, *Submitted*, *Verified*, *Needs work* or *Missed*, the modules ticked in that period, and the reviewer's comment.
- **Submit update** (or *Submit late* for a missed period): what was completed (required), hours spent (required), confidence 1–5, blockers, plan for the next period. After *Needs work*, **Update & resend**.
- **Feedback from mentor & manager**: every comment from check-in reviews plus the mentor's and manager's **checkpoint reviews** (average rating, keep doing / start doing), named and dated, newest first.
- Modules by week with ticks, and the checkpoint reviews list.

## Mentor and manager

- The mentor and manager get an in-app notification when an update is submitted.
- **Review** opens the update with the modules ticked in that period; **✓ Verify** (comment optional) or **Needs work** (comment required). The trainee is notified.
- Mentors see **People I mentor** on their own training page with how many updates wait for review.
- The manager's **Trainees & assignments** table shows check-ins as verified/total with *to review* and *missed* counts and the cadence.

## Signals

- A **missed** period counts against the plan and shows in red; repeated misses are a 1:1 topic.
- Confidence ≤ 2 or a blocker in an update is worth a same-week conversation.
